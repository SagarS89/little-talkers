Getting more English out of it

  • Sit with them when you can and say the words back. At three and four, children pick up most of their language from talking with people; the clips give you something to talk about.
  • Let a clip finish and press Again. Hearing the same song or phrase several times is how it sticks.
  • Use the talk-time cards. A child who answers out loud is practising speaking, not only listening.
